Hi! I am a second-year PhD student at PAttern Recognition and NEural Computing (PARNEC) group, NUAA where I am fortunate enough to be advised by [Prof. Songcan Chen](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=SdPinGIAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN) who is also the leader of [PARNEC](https://parnec.nuaa.edu.cn/) group.

My research interests lie broadly in Machine Learning, especially Continual Learning Theory and Weakly Supervised Learning – check my [publications](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=3juyXgQAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN) or [CV](cv.pdf) for more information. I am open to other topics, so feel free to reach out if you’d like to chat!
